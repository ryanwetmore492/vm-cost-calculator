Calculation settings

Assumptions: storage billed on provisioned disk. Tiers priced per TB convert GB → TB by dividing by 1024; tiers priced per GB use provisioned GB directly with no conversion (currently: all storage tiers priced per TB). Compute and VMware licensing are billed per GB of configured RAM. Windows SPLA is a flat per-VM charge applied when the OS string contains “windows” (case-insensitive). All figures are monthly USD.
